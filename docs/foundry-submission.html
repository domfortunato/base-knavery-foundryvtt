Base Knavery is an unofficial, fan-made system for Knave, first and second editions. Knave plays out of the box; Knave 2e works with your own copy of the book.

What you get

  • A complete system that runs Knave (the first edition) out of the box, with its CC BY 4.0 rules included.
  • Full Knave 2e support, with rich content, when you provide your copy of the Knave 2e PDF.
  • Mix the two editions' rules, one switch per rule.
  • Instant random character creation for PCs, NPCs and monsters.
  • A random spell generator.
  • A bestiary, and more than 2,900 pieces of human-made art (portraits, tokens and icons).
  • A Game Master's Dashboard: requests, damage, time and hazards, the party, XP.
  • Fully customizable marketplace, backgrounds, weapons, gear, spells, magic items and potions.
  • GM macros for importing additional tables, quickly turning features on and off, and more.
  • A complete guide, in the compendium, that explains every feature.
  • Translation-ready: the interface and Knave 1e rules; translators welcome.

Base Knavery is an independent production of Dom Bosco and is not affiliated with Questing Beast LLC. Source, licences and issues on GitHub.
