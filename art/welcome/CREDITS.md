# Welcome window art

art (`<name>.png`, the light theme) and as her white-filled version (`<name>-white.png`, the dark theme).
© Lydia Comer, used in Base Knavery's welcome window with her permission, which covers cropping them.

`art/lydia-comer/`. Do not reuse them outside Base Knavery without asking the artist.

Each file is cropped square to the head and shoulders from the artist's original and shrunk by a whole
factor (3, or 4 for the jester); nothing is redrawn or recoloured. Sources, from her files:
