Knave 2e compatibility
----------------------

Base Knavery is an independent production of Dom Bosco and is not affiliated
with Questing Beast LLC.

It is compatible with Knave 2e under the Knave: Second Edition Third Party
License, Version 1.0. That licence permits reuse of the general concepts, rules
and game mechanics of Knave: Second Edition. It does not permit verbatim
passages of its text or its art, and this system ships none: every Knave 2e
table ships as an empty shell for the Game Master to fill, and the bestiary
carries statistics only. (The filled tables are Knave first edition's, below.) The Knave: Second Edition logo and the Questing Beast
logo are not used anywhere in this system.

This system contains no text from Dungeon Crawl Classics.


Knave — CC BY 4.0
-----------------

Knave (version 1.0) by Ben Milton, the game's first edition, is licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Its rules are set out in this system as Foundry automation and settings (the
Knave 1e choices under Configure Rules): ability scores as the lowest of 3d6,
the starting kit, death at below 0 HP, healing, spellbooks once a day,
advancement and group initiative. Changes: they are adapted as automation and
settings, 1e armor is expressed as armor points, and each can be mixed with the
Knave 2e rules.

These tables in the tables compendium are from the same work:
  - the ten character traits (Physique, Face, Skin, Hair, Clothing, Virtue,
    Vice, Speech, Background, Misfortune) and Alignment;
  - Reaction;
  - the starting gear (Starting Armor, Helmets and Shields, Dungeoneering
    Gear, General Gear 1 and 2);
  - Spells (Knave 1e), the 100 level-less spells, with the book's note on
    what "L" means.
They are reproduced unchanged, except that "Misfortunes" is named
Misfortune, the armor table is named Starting Armor, and the spells' note is
shortened. Knave's first edition is a
different work from Knave: Second Edition, whose text this system does not
ship.
